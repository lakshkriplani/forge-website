Where your data lives

On your phone. Full stop.

Forge is offline-first by design. Your workouts, body data, photos, and diet are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and we never sell your data.

Two features are optional and use the internet only if you turn them on: Jam group workouts, which need an email sign-in, and reading your health data, which is read-only.

Questions, answered.

Is Forge free?
Yes. The core app, including workout tracking, the exercise library, analytics, and nutrition planning, is free to use.
Do I need an account?
No. You can install Forge and track everything without ever signing in. An email sign-in is only needed for the optional Jam feature, where you train live with friends.
Does my data sync to the cloud?
No. Your workouts, body data, photos, and diet are stored locally on your phone and are not uploaded to any server. You can export a backup file yourself at any time, and you decide where it goes.
Which devices does it run on?
Forge runs on Android and iOS, from the same app. Both versions are in final testing ahead of the App Store and Google Play release.
Does it work with my smartwatch?
Yes. On Android it reads your watch data through Health Connect, and on iOS through Apple Health. Steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, and calories are pulled from the watch and shown in the Health tab. Forge only reads this data, it never changes it.
What is Jam?
Jam lets you and your friends work out together live. Share a code, and everyone sees each other's sets, personal records, and a live leaderboard in real time. It is completely optional and off unless you start or join a Jam.
