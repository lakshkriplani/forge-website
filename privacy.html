Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 August 2026 Last updated: 14 August 2026 Version 2.0

This policy explains what information the Forge app ("Forge", "the app") processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. Forge is built to be private by default: the vast majority of your data never leaves your device.

The short version

  • ✓Your training data stays on your phone. Workouts, body metrics, photos, and nutrition are stored locally, not on our servers.
  • ✓No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • ✓Two features are optional and online: Jam group workouts (needs an email sign-in) and reading your health/watch data (read-only).
  • ✓You can delete your data and your Jam account at any time, and export a copy whenever you want.

01Who we are

Forge is an offline-first workout and nutrition tracking app for Android and iOS. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, the data controller for the limited personal data described below is the developer and operator of Forge, Laksh Kriplani ("we", "us", "our").

You can reach us about privacy at any time: lakshkriplani188@gmail.com.

02Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Forge mobile app on Android (via Google Play) and iOS (via the Apple App Store), and to this website. It does not apply to third-party services you choose to connect, such as Google Health Connect, Apple Health, or your device operating system, each of which is governed by its own privacy policy.

03Information we process

3.1 Data stored only on your device

The core of Forge works entirely offline. The following is created by you and stored in the app's local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot see it:

  • Workouts, routines, exercises, sets, reps, weights, RPE, and rest times
  • Personal records and training history
  • Body measurements, body weight, and progress photos
  • Nutrition, calories, macros, meal plans, grocery lists, and water intake
  • Goals, achievements, app settings, and preferences

3.2 Account information (Jam only)

Jam is an optional feature that lets you train live with other people. If, and only if, you choose to create or join a Jam, you sign in with an email address and a password. We use this to create and authenticate your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored in hashed form; we never see your plain-text password. If you never use Jam, no account is created and none of this data exists.

3.3 Live workout data shared during a Jam

While you are actively in a Jam, the workout progress you choose to share is transmitted so the other members of that same Jam can see it in real time. Depending on your Jam share settings, this may include completed sets, reps, weights, personal records, and pace. You control what is shared in the app's Jam settings. This data is associated with your Jam account for the duration of the session.

3.4 Health & fitness data (optional)

If you enable the Health tab, Forge can read health and fitness data from Google Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) with your explicit, separate permission. See Section 5 for the full commitments that apply to this data. The categories we may read are:

  • Steps
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Sleep
  • Active and total calories burned

This access is read-only. The values are displayed in the app and stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us or any third party.

Forge contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no tracking technologies. 05Health & fitness data: our commitments

Health data is sensitive, and both Apple and Google apply extra rules to it. We follow them, and we go further where we can:

  • Read-only. Forge only reads health data. It never writes, changes, or deletes anything in Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Stays on your device. Health data pulled from Health Connect or Apple Health is shown in the app and stored locally. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Never for advertising or marketing. We do not use health or fitness data for targeted advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we never sell it.
  • Not shared. We do not transfer your health data to any third party, data broker, or ad network.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit): we do not store any HealthKit-derived health information in iCloud, and we use it only to display your metrics inside the app, consistent with the App Store Review Guidelines.
  • Google Health Connect: our use of Health Connect data is limited to features that are visible to you in the app's Health tab, consistent with Google Play's Health Connect permissions and User Data policies. We request only the health permissions the Health tab needs.
  • You are in control. You can withdraw health access at any time in Health Connect (Android) or the Health app (iOS), and delete the locally stored copy by clearing app data or uninstalling.

06How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Personal data is only disclosed in these limited situations:

Service providers (sub-processors)

The optional Jam feature is powered by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), which provides authentication and database hosting on our behalf as a data processor. When you use Jam, your email, hashed password, and live Jam session data are processed on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase processes this data only to provide the service to us and under its own security and privacy commitments. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Other Jam participants

The live workout data you choose to share in a Jam (3.3) is visible to the other members of that Jam while the session is active. Only share Jams with people you trust.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

Business transfers

If the app or its assets are ever transferred to another party, personal data may be part of that transfer; we will require the recipient to honour this policy or notify you of any material change.

07International data transfers

The local data on your device stays in your country because it stays on your phone. For the optional Jam feature, our processor Supabase may store and process account and session data on servers located outside your country, including in other jurisdictions. Where personal data of individuals in the EEA or UK is transferred internationally, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. By using Jam, you understand your account data may be processed in this way.

08Data retention

Local data: kept on your device until you delete it, reset the app's data, or uninstall the app. Because it is not on our servers, we cannot access or recover it for you, so keep your own backups.

Jam account and session data: your account is kept until you delete it (see Section 11). Live Jam session data is transient and is cleaned up automatically after sessions end. When you delete your account, associated account data is deleted, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.

Health data: stored locally only; removed when you clear app data or uninstall.

09Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. Traffic between the app and our Jam backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider. Because most of your data lives on your device, its security also depends on your device: use a screen lock and keep your operating system up to date. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over the personal data we hold about you (primarily your Jam account data, since local data is already under your direct control on your device).

  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to erasure")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your data
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email lakshkriplani188@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by law (generally within 30 days). We may need to verify your request using the email associated with your account.

11Account & data deletion

You are always in control of your data.

Delete your on-device data

Use Settings → Reset all data in the app, or uninstall the app, to permanently remove all locally stored workouts, nutrition, photos, and health data from your device.

Delete your Jam account

If you created a Jam account, you can request its deletion in either of these ways:

  • In the app: open the Jam or account settings and choose Delete account.
  • By email: send a deletion request from your account email to lakshkriplani188@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Forge account".

When you delete your account, we delete your account record and associated personal data (such as your email and any residual Jam session data) within 30 days. We may retain a minimal amount of information where we are legally required to, or to prevent fraud and abuse; such data is deleted once no longer needed. Deleting your account does not remove data stored locally on your device, which you control separately as described above.

12Children's privacy

Forge is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a Jam account, contact us and we will delete it.
